Data Deletion Instructions — Blink ESS WhatsApp Assistant

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Users of the Blink ESS WhatsApp Assistant may request deletion of personal information associated with their use of the service.

How to Request Deletion

To request deletion, contact andrewning@blinkess.com with the subject line WhatsApp Assistant Data Deletion Request.

Please provide your WhatsApp phone number, including the country code, and any information reasonably necessary to identify the relevant records. Do not include passwords or other sensitive credentials.

What Happens Next

Blink ESS will review the request, verify the requester's identity where necessary, and delete personal information that is eligible for deletion from systems under its control.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law, for legitimate security purposes, or to meet applicable legal obligations. If information cannot be deleted in full, we will explain the applicable reason where required.

Information held by third-party services may also be subject to their own deletion processes and policies.

Contact

For deletion requests or questions about personal information, contact:

Blink ESS
Email: andrewning@blinkess.com
