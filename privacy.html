Privacy Policy — Blink ESS WhatsApp Assistant

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Organization: Blink ESS

Contact: andrewning@blinkess.com

1. Purpose

Blink ESS operates a WhatsApp-based assistant for internal business communications and productivity. The assistant is intended for authorized employees and other approved users.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with the assistant, we may process:

  • WhatsApp phone numbers, profile information, and message content.
  • Files, images, documents, and other information you choose to send.
  • Message history and technical information necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.
  • Information retrieved from or submitted to business systems when you request an action through the assistant.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to respond to messages, perform requested business tasks, retrieve relevant company information, maintain service security, and troubleshoot or improve the assistant.

4. AI Processing and Third-Party Services

Messages may be processed by Blink ESS's internally hosted software and AI models, as well as external services or integrations required to fulfil a request. These may include WhatsApp services operated by Meta Platforms and authorized business systems connected to the assistant.

Information is shared only as reasonably necessary to operate the service, fulfil requests, maintain security, or meet legal obligations.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Messages and related information may be retained in application logs, conversation history, or connected business systems, depending on the services used. Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for operational, security, business, or legal purposes, subject to applicable retention requirements.

6. Security

Blink ESS uses appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Access and Disclosure

Access to the assistant and associated information is intended to be limited to authorized users and personnel. We do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed to service providers supporting the assistant or where required by law.

8. Your Choices and Requests

Do not send passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information unless it is necessary and you are authorized to do so. For questions about personal information, access, correction, or deletion requests, contact us using the address below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the service or its data practices change. The current version will be published at this URL, with the effective date updated as appropriate.
